Project of Innovation

Zero Methane Solution

Raven Tech Systems delivers proven, field-tested technology designed to help oil and gas operators comply with federal methane emission regulations and avoid costly penalties.
Following the passage of the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022, Congress established the Methane Emissions Reduction Program (MERP) under Section 136 of the Clean Air Act. This law imposes a Waste Emissions Charge (WEC)—a fine levied on operators who exceed methane emission thresholds set by the EPA. Beginning at $900 per metric ton of excess methane in 2024, the penalty increases to $1,200 in 2025 and $1,500 in 2026 and beyond, posing serious financial risk for non-compliance.
Raven Tech Systems has engineered a complete solution to help operators reduce emissions and meet compliance targets.
Our technology stack includes:
  • Solar-Powered Yellow Stands designed to deliver reliable, self-sustaining power in remote field environments—enabling full conversion from methane-fueled systems to clean electric operation without the need for grid connectivity.
  • Zero Methane™️ Software Suite, a proprietary control platform that provides real-time monitoring, alerts, and custom automation routines to eliminate fugitive emissions and meet site-specific operational requirements.
  • Integration & Support tailored to your operations, ensuring seamless deployment and configuration across all locations, with scalable architecture to support expansion and regulatory updates.
Our systems are already deployed in the field, delivering measurable reductions in methane output and empowering operators to proactively avoid federal penalties. With Raven Tech Systems, you’re not just adopting technology—you’re securing a compliance pathway that protects your bottom line and positions your operation for long-term environmental responsibility.
